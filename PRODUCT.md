# PRODUCT

## Purpose
Showcase Ade Ramdani as a high-tier NOC Engineer capable of managing critical infrastructure with absolute reliability.

## Users
- Technical recruiters looking for experienced infrastructure staff.
- Engineering managers who value stability and proactiveness.
- Fellow engineers looking for technical expertise.

## Brand Identity
- **Stable**: The bedrock of the network.
- **Sharp**: Quick response, precise troubleshooting.
- **Technical**: Deep understanding of protocols and hardware.
- **Anti-Slop**: Every element has a reason. No generic "modern" gradients just for the sake of it.

## Tone
Technical, direct, authoritative but approachable. No buzzwords.

## Register
brand

## Anti-references
- SaaS landing pages with generic wavy backgrounds.
- Icon + Header + Text card grids.
- "Modern" dark modes that are just pure black #000.
